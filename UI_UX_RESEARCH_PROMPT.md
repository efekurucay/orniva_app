# 🎨 UI/UX Deep Research Agent Prompt for Orniva App

## 📋 Research Mission

You are a **UI/UX Research Agent** tasked with conducting comprehensive research to redesign and improve the **Orniva Bird Identification App**. Your goal is to provide detailed, actionable recommendations for colors, design patterns, user experience flows, and visual aesthetics that will make the app **modern, intuitive, and delightful** to use.

- What colors work well for:
  - Primary actions (identify bird, capture photo)

- Gradient suggestions if applicable
- Real-world examples from successful nature/bird apps
